Reading the Data Carefully

The trustee's reported annual compensation was $9,000 in 2025, the same as in 2019 through 2024. The three board entries totaled $2,100 in 2025.

No separately reported deputy or clerk compensation entry appears in the reviewed data after 2014. This does not establish whether a deputy, volunteer, contractor, or statutory designee served in any later year.
