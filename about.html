About This Project

The Jackson Township Transparency Project organizes public information so residents can better understand local government and participate in civic life.

Mission

We aim to explain township responsibilities, connect residents with public records, present reported financial data accurately, and provide neutral election information. We do not endorse or oppose candidates or political parties.

Editorial Principles

  • Source first: link to official records where practical.
  • Separate facts and questions: identify what documents establish and what remains unverified.
  • Apply consistent standards: use the same approach regardless of the officials or candidates involved.
  • Correct errors: update content when better evidence becomes available.
  • Respect privacy: publish only information relevant to the site's public education purpose.

What This Site Is Not

This website is not operated by Jackson Township government, the DeKalb County election office, or a political campaign. It does not offer legal advice or replace official public records.

Information about a filing date, compensation entry, or state examination should not be interpreted as an allegation of misconduct without evidence supporting that conclusion.

Primary Sources

  • Indiana Gateway for compensation and reporting data.
  • Indiana State Board of Accounts for examinations.
  • Indiana General Assembly for state law.
  • Indiana Election Division for election procedures.
  • Jackson Township government website for local statements.

Project Independence

This is an independently maintained educational website. It does not claim to speak for any elected official or candidate. Readers are encouraged to review the original sources and participate in public meetings.
