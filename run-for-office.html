How to Run for Township Office

Township elections give eligible residents a way to participate in local government. This guide explains the general process and directs prospective candidates to official election authorities.

Information only. Filing requirements and deadlines must be confirmed with election officials.

Which Offices?

Township trustee: administers township executive functions and statutory services.

Township advisory board: three elected members carry out statutory budget and fiscal responsibilities.

These township offices ordinarily follow a four-year election cycle, including 2022 and 2026.

General Steps

  1. Confirm office eligibility and residency requirements with the DeKalb County Clerk.
  2. Review current election calendars, ballot access rules, and filing forms.
  3. Determine the applicable filing path, such as a major-party primary or another legally available route.
  4. Submit required documents before the official deadlines.
  5. Review campaign finance requirements and reporting obligations, if applicable.
  6. Learn the duties of the office and communicate respectfully with residents.

Independent and minor-party filing procedures can differ from major-party procedures. Do not assume the same deadline applies to every candidate.

Official Election Resources
