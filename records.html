2021–2025 State Compliance Engagement

The Indiana State Board of Accounts (SBOA) publishes examination and compliance engagement reports for local government units. Such reports must be read in full, including the examination scope, findings, responses, and dates, before drawing conclusions.

Meeting Minutes and Other Township Records

Minutes, meeting notices, budgets, resolutions, and service agreements can help residents understand decisions and their context. Not finding a record on a website does not establish that the record is missing or that a legal duty was violated.

Residents may request existing public records from the government office that maintains them under Indiana's Access to Public Records Act, subject to applicable exceptions.
